## Checklist

to be read by the team leader prior to delivery, after OB sign-out and once the bed is prepared.

1. Members state their own names and roles

2. Plan
   ➔ Diagnosis  
   ➔ EFW, GA  
   ➔ Cord clamping cut at 10cm TELL OB  

   **Gastroschisis**  
   ➔ Place baby in bag up to axilla and tip toward right side  
   ➔ **DO NOT BAG MASK**  
   ➔ Intubate if apneic/HR <100 or providing CPAP >5 min  
   ➔ Use meconium aspirator if there is mec stained fluid and no chest rise with PPV via ETT  
   ➔ TL 1- constantly monitor color/perfusion of bowel, place replogle after intubation (or before return to NICU)  
   **Complications:**  
   ➔ Bowel dusky or kinked  
     Keep sterile while repositioning  
   ➔ Unable to intubate  
     Place LMA  

   **Omphalocele**  
   ➔ Place baby in bag up to axilla  
   ➔ May give mask PPV  

3. Equipment check
   ➔ Pedi cap in-line  
   ➔ ETT and blade prepped  
   ➔ Abdominal Wall Defect supply bag  
   ➔ IO drill  

4. Communication check
   ➔ Use closed loop communication  
   ➔ If you see something, say something  
   ➔ Any concerns?
