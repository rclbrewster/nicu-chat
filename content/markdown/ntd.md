## Checklist

to be read by the team leader prior to delivery, after OB sign-out and once the bed is prepared.

1. Members state their own names and roles

2. Plan
   - ➔ Diagnosis: Neural Tube Defect (repaired or unrepaired)
   - ➔ EFW, GA
   - ➔ Cord clamping strategy
   - ➔ Notify OB to keep baby on their side
   - ➔ Left side down on the warmer
   - ➔ TL1- cover defect with moist Telfa and monitor
   - ➔ **If apneic or gasping- provide PPV with LMA- do not use face mask**

   **Complications:**
   - ➔ Rupture of sac
     - ● Continue resuscitation
     - ● Keep sterile
     - ● Re-evaluate in NICU
   - ➔ Unable to ventilate with LMA
     - ● Intubate supine w/ donut

3. Equipment check
   - ➔ LMA on bed
   - ➔ Pedi cap in-line
   - ➔ Moistened telfa
   - ➔ Donut

4. Communication check
   - ➔ Use closed loop communication
   - ➔ If you see something, say something
   - ➔ Any concerns?
