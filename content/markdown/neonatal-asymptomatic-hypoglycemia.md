# Neonatal Asymptomatic Hypoglycemia Screening & Management Pathway
*for Infants 35 weeks Gestational Age and Greater*

## INCLUSION CRITERIA:
Asymptomatic Infants with any of the following conditions:
- Small for gestational age infant (SGA)
- Large for gestational age infant (LGA)
- Infant of a diabetic mother (IDM)
- Infant between 35-36 6/7 weeks gestational age
- Infant identified at the discretion of provider

## EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
- Signs or symptoms of hypoglycemia (See page 2)
- >48 hours old
- Unable to feed
- <35 weeks Gestational age

## PATH A: INITIAL FEED AFTER BIRTH
- Feed within 1 hour of birth and continue to feed Q2-3 hours
- Check POC glucose 30 minutes AFTER feed is initiated, no later than 90 minutes after birth; AND prior to transfer to Maternity
- If feeding attempt unsuccessful, check POC glucose no later than 90 minutes after birth AND prior to transfer to Maternity
- For 2nd or 3rd dose of gel, supplement BDM/Formula if colostrum <5ml

| Glucose Level | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| <25           | 1st dose of dextrose gel & feed 5-10ml |
| 25-34         | 1st dose of dextrose gel & breastfeed (No supplementation) |
| ≥35           | Path A Complete |

- Recheck POC glucose 1 hour after dextrose gel

### If <25:
- Give Dextrose Gel & feed 5-10 mls
- Notify MD STAT
- Transfer to NN
- STAT Serum Glucose

### If 25-34:
- Notify Provider
- FINAL dose of dextrose gel & feed. Supplement 5-10mls if colostrum <5ml

- Re-check POC & Serum glucose 1 hour after gel administration

### If ≥35:
- Path A Complete

## PATH B: INITIATE ≥4 HOURS OLD
- Check AC POC glucose no sooner than 2 hours from last check.
- For 2nd or 3rd dose of gel, supplement BDM/Formula if colostrum <5ml

| Glucose Level | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| <45           | Did patient receive TOTAL of 3 doses of dextrose gel? |
| ≥45           | Path B Complete |

### If NO:
- Give dextrose gel & feed 5-10mls
- Notify MD
- Transfer to NN

### If YES:
- STOP Path B & Notify MD
- STAT serum glucose
- Consider transfer to ICN and IV glucose

## REPEAT Pathway B until following Criteria is met:
### PATHWAY COMPLETION CRITERIA
- For all LGA and IDM:
  - 3 consecutive AC blood glucose ≥45
- SGA and Late Preterm Infants:
  - 3 consecutive AC blood glucose ≥45 AND at least one AC blood glucose ≥45 documented after 24 hours of life

# Purpose: Glucose screening is required for newborns that are high risk of asymptomatic hypoglycemia

## Table 1: SGA and LGA Weight Cutoffs

| Gestational Age | SGA    | LGA    |
|-----------------|--------|--------|
| ≤36 6/7         | ALL NEONATES SHOULD BE SCREENED |
| 37 0/7 to 37 6/7| < 2400g| > 3600g|
| 38 0/7 to 38 6/7| < 2600g| > 3800g|
| 39 0/7 to 39 6/7| < 2700g| > 4000g|
| 40 0/7 to 41 6/7| < 2900g| > 4100g|

## Table 2: Dextrose gel (40%) Dosing

| Birthweight (kg) | Dextrose Gel (mL) |
|------------------|-------------------|
| <2.5 kg          | 1 mL              |
| 2.5-3.49 kg      | 1.5 mL            |
| 3.5-4.49 kg      | 2 mL              |
| ≥4.5 kg          | 2.5 mL            |

### Dextrose Gel:

When indicated, administer weight-based dose (Table 2) of 40% Dextrose Gel by rubbing with a gloved finger on both sides of the infant’s buccal mucosa prior to feeding the infant. A maximum of 3 doses of glucose gel may be administered in 24 hours.

### Feeds:

Breastfeeding is the preferred method of feeding. If unable to breastfeed effectively, help the mother express and provide colostrum or breast milk by an alternative method. If colostrum is minimal and glucose is <25 or infant is getting 2nd or 3rd dose of dextrose gel, supplement with 5-10 mL donor human milk or formula. Notify provider immediately for any hypoglycemic infant that is unable to feed.

### For large for gestation (LGA) and infants of a diabetic mother

After passing initial glucose screen (Path A), screening must continue when patient is ≥4 hours of age (Path B) until 3 consecutive prior to feed (AC) blood glucose checks are ≥ 45 mg/dL. See Table 1 for LGA birth weight cutoffs by gestational age.

### For small for gestation (SGA) & late preterm infants (gestational age 35 0/7 weeks to 36 6/7 weeks)

After passing initial glucose screen (Path A), screening must continue when patient is ≥4 hours of age (Path B) until 3 consecutive AC blood glucose checks are ≥ 45 AND at least one AC blood glucose ≥ 45 after 24 hours of life. Contact provider if <25 or <45 and received 3 doses of dextrose gel. Do NOT restart pathway B. See Table 1 for SGA birth weight cutoffs by gestational age.

## Point of Care (POC) Blood Glucoses

In PATH A, the first check should be obtained 30 minutes after the initiation of the first feed, no later than 90 minutes of life AND prior to transfer to Maternity. The priority is checking prior to 90 minutes of life, including for infants who do not feed. In the first 4 hours of life, a glucose of 35 or above is normal. If the first glucose check is in the normal range, infant should be encouraged to feed every 2-3 hours and does not need another AC check until after 4 hours of age. In PATH B, routine checks are obtained prior to feeds (AC). Unless otherwise stated, all POC glucose rechecks should be timed for 1 hour after administration of dextrose gel and the start of the feed.

## STAT Serum Glucoses

POC blood glucoses can be inaccurate at low values. Hypoglycemia that could require an IV glucose intervention should be confirmed with a STAT serum lab sample. Never delay administration of glucose, a feeding, or further POC checks while working to obtain a STAT serum glucose.

## REMINDER: Symptomatic hypoglycemia requires immediate intervention!

The signs and symptoms of hypoglycemia are non-specific and include

- Tremors, jitteriness, irritability
- Exaggerated Moro reflex
- Weak or high pitched cry
- Lethargy, listlessness, hypotonia
- Cyanosis, apnea, tachypnea
- Hypothermia, temperature instability
- Poor suck, refusal to feed

## Signs or symptoms of Hypoglycemia

- Immediately check POC glucose
- Notify provider

# Dextrose Gel Critical Shortage Addendum

March 15, 2022

Whenever dextrose gel is unavailable for treatment of hypoglycemia substitute with 5-10mL* **Donor Human Milk or Formula**.

*RN may increase volume of DHM/Formula supplementation as tolerated if infant continues to have hypoglycemia despite previous treatment.*
