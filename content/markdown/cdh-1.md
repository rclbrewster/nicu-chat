## Checklist

to be read by the team leader prior to delivery, after OB sign-out and once the bed is prepared.

1. Members state their own names and roles

2. Plan
   - ➔ Diagnosis: CDH - discuss laterality
   - ➔ EFW, GA
   - ➔ Plan for immediate cord clamping
   - ➔ Do **NOT** bag mask
   - ➔ Intubate immediately by most experienced provider
   - ➔ Initial PPV  
     R 40-60bpm 20/5 iT 0.3-0.4
   - ➔ PPV > 5 minutes  
     Faster rates and shorter I-times may be required if patient is unstable
   - ➔ Goal saturations per NRP but may tolerate > 70% for 1 hour post-birth to avoid using high pressures
   - ➔ TL 1 - place replogle after intubation

   **Complications:**
   - ➔ Unable to intubate
     - ● Place LMA
   - ➔ Pneumothorax
     - ● Identify provider to decompress air – midline anterior 2nd intercostal space
     - ● Consider returning to the NICU to confirm with CXR if patient stable

3. Equipment check
   - ➔ Pedi cap in-line and FiO2 50%
   - ➔ ETT and blade prepped
   - ➔ Feeding tube and syringe
   - ➔ LMA on bed

4. Communication check
   - ➔ Use closed loop communication
   - ➔ If you see something, say something
   - ➔ Any concerns?
