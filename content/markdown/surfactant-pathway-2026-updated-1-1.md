*[Flowchart: Surfactant Pathway for Preterm Infants <32 weeks]*

Surfactant Pathway  
Preterm Infants <32 weeks

- Infant intubated in delivery room or upon NICU admission
  - Chest x-ray after intubation
    - FiO2 <30%
      - <30 weeks gestation
      - >30 weeks gestation
        - Consider removal of the ETT
    - FiO2 ≥30%
      - Administer surfactant
        - Surfactant to be administered within the golden hour for infants <30 weeks

- Admit to NICU on bubble CPAP +5
  - 30 minutes later
    - Assess vital signs and respiratory status
      - FiO2 <30%
        - Continue present management on CPAP +5 with continual assessment of vital signs and respiratory status.
      - FiO2 ≥30% or worsening WOB
        - Increase pressure to CPAP +6
          - 30 minutes later
            - Reassess vital signs and respiratory status
              - FiO2 <30%
                - Continue present management on CPAP +6 with continual assessment of vital signs and respiratory status.
              - FiO2 ≥30% or worsening WOB
                - Increase pressure to CPAP +7
                  - 30 minutes later
                    - Reassess vital signs and respiratory status
                      - FiO2 <30%
                        - Continue present management on CPAP +7. Notify provider of increased FiO2 or worsening WOB.
                      - FiO2 30-59%
                        - <24+6 weeks or <750g
                          - Intubate, obtain CXR
                            - Administer surfactant via ETT if consistent with RDS
                        - ≥25 weeks
                          - Administer surfactant following LISA/SALSA protocol
                            - LISA 25 to 27+6 weeks 750-1250g BW
                            - SALSA ≥28 weeks and >1250g

AAP strongly recommends surfactant administration to preterm infants born <30 weeks requiring mechanical ventilation

Considerations for additional surfactant doses:
- Persistent high FiO2 requirement and increased WOB
- Continued need for significant mechanical ventilation support
