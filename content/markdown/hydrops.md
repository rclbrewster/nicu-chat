## Checklist

to be read by the team leader prior to delivery, after OB sign-out and once the bed is prepared

1. Members state their own names and roles

2. Plan
   ➔ Primary Diagnosis  
   ➔ EFW (hydropic and true weight), GA  
   ➔ Cord clamping strategy  
   ➔ Identify sites of fluid collection and degree of severity (minimal to large)  
   ➔ Consider using anesthesia bag for higher pressures  
   ➔ Intubation by experienced provider  
   ➔ Bring emergency blood for immune hydrops or severe anemia  
   ➔ Consider high FiO2 if anemic  

   **Complications:**
   ➔ Significant airway edema  
     ● Smaller ETT available  
   ➔ Unable to obtain chest rise and/or HR >100 after successful intubation  
     ● Paracentesis and/or thoracentesis and laterality  
   ➔ Volume depletion or poor oxygenation despite ventilation  
     ● UVC placement, blood or NS  
   ➔ Pneumothorax  
     ● Thoracentesis  
   ➔ Pericardial effusion  
     ● Alert Cardiology for stat Echo in the unit  

3. Equipment check
   ➔ Pedi cap in-line  
   ➔ ETT prepped expected size (2) and one size smaller (2), blade prepped  
   ➔ Thoracentesis/Paracentesis supplies prepped  
   ➔ UVC prepped  
   ➔ Blood, NS, Epi doses  

4. Communication check
   ➔ Use closed loop communication  
   ➔ If you see something, say something  
   ➔ Any concerns?
