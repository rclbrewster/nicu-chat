# Infant (1501-2000 gram) Feeding Pathway

**Pathway Purpose:** To reduce practice variation for feeding infants in the intermediate care nursery (ICN) and neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) to accelerate feeding advancement, promote GI maturity, and improve overall enteral nutrition.

## Inclusion Criteria:
- ≥32+0/7 weeks gestation
- Birthweight 1501-2000 grams
- Clinically ready to initiate enteral feeds
- Admitted to ICN or NICU

## Exclusion Criteria:
- Major congenital anomalies
- Ill / septic infant
- Infant requiring abdominal surgery

## Feeding advance
- "Day 1" is first day infant is ready to feed
- Advance every 24h, not calendar days
- Move to left side of pathway if needing NG tube

## Is the infant vigorous, showing feeding cues, and ready to feed orally?

- **No**

  - **Place NGT (see next page)**
    - **Day 1**
      - Total feed volume: 20ml/kg/d
      - Feeding frequency q 3 hours

    - **Day 2**
      - Total feed volume: 60ml/kg/d

    - **Day 3***
      - Total feed volume: 100 ml/kg/d
      - *Begin Fortification: 24 cal/oz*

    - **Day 4**
      - Total feed volume: 140 ml/kg/d

    - **Day 5**
      - Total feed volume: 160ml/kg/d
      - Start Multivitamins & Iron

    - **Day 6 +**
      - Total feed volume: 160ml/kg/d or more
      - Adjust volumes based on weight gain

- **Yes**

  - **May attempt to ad lib feed**
    - **Day 1**
      - Total feed volume: 20ml/kg/d
      - Target volume per feed: 5 mL × 8 feeds (frequency q3)

    - **Day 2**
      - Total feed volume: 60ml/kg/d
      - Target volume per feed: 7.5 mL/kg/feeding

    - **Day 3***
      - Total feed volume: 100 ml/kg/d
      - Target volume per feed: 12.5 mL/kg/feeding
      - *Begin Fortification: 24 cal/oz*

    - **Day 4**
      - Total feed volume: 140 ml/kg/d
      - Target volume per feed: 17.5 mL/kg/feeding

    - **Day 5**
      - Total feed volume: 160ml/kg/d
      - Target feed volume: 20 mL/kg/feeding
      - Start Multivitamins & Iron

    - **Day 6 +**
      - Total feed volume: 160+ml/kg/d or more
      - Adjust volumes based on weight gain

# Infant (1501-2000 gram) Feeding Pathway

## Additional Considerations

### NG Tube Feeding

**Consider Placement of Nasogastric tube:**
- Lethargic
- Poor feeding cues
- Hypoglycemia
- Unable to meet feeding goals in 12-24 hours

When placing NG, move to left side of pathway

**PO + NG feedings**
- Refer to Infant Driven Feeding Protocol

**Removal of NGT**
- Consider once taking 80-85% feeds PO in 24h

### Adjunct therapies

**IV fluids**

It is not necessary to routinely start IVF or PPN in this patient population.
- Reasons to start:
  - Persistent hypoglycemia
  - Respiratory support
- Starting IVF: D10W @60-80 ml/kg/day.

**Vitamins and Iron**
- See table on next page for specific dosing recommendations based on gestational age, type of milk / formula, and weight

### Definitions

- DHM – Donor Human Milk
- EHM – Expressed Human Milk
- HMF – human milk fortifier
- PPN – Peripheral Parenteral Nutrition
- BWt – birthweight

### Nutrition

**Choice of Milk**
- Breastmilk (expressed or donor) EHM/DHM
- If family declines DHM, use transitional 22 cal/ounce formula
  - Transitional Formula examples: Neosure, Enfacare
- Stopping DHM: > 2 weeks of age & >34 weeks PMA or preparing for discharge (or if mom does not plan to BF)

**Fortification**

Early fortification improves developmental outcomes and improves growth.
- Start on Day 3 - Fortify EHM/DHM or formula to 24kcal
- Confer with RD on when to discontinue

**Breastfeeding**
- See Lactation Risk Standards
- Encourage birth parent to hand express or pump 8x/day
- Start with 1-2 direct breastfeeds/day and increase based on baby's ability

### Growth

**Expected weight loss**
- Max 7% of BWt

**Expected weight gain**
- Regain BWt by 7-10 days
  - <2000 grams: 15-20 grams average/day
  - ≥2000 grams 20-30 grams average/day
  - OFC/Length: 0.5-1 cm/week

### Discharge Planning

- Home feeding plans based on maternal milk production, infant growth, and breastfeeding ability.
- Goal is unfortified EHM at least 170-180 ml/kg/day
  - If poor weight gain on full feeds, fortify to 22 cal/ounce or 2-3 bottles transitional formula (22 cal/oz) per day
- Transition to home feeding plan at 35 weeks or ready for discharge in 2-3 days.
  - Adequate EHM+Good growth: EHM ad lib
  - Adequate EHM+Slow Growth: EHM + 2 bottles Neosure 22/day
  - Inadequate EHM: EHM + Neosure 22 or 24 as needed
- Be sure to incorporate breastfeeding frequency and timing in home feeding plan

## Additional Notes

- **Fortification:**
  - Fortify MBM/DBM to 24 kcal/oz with HMF
  - OR use preterm formula 24 kcal

- **When:**
  - Minimum 9-10 feeds/day (BF/MBM/DBM)
  - 2-3 feeds/day of post-discharge formula (22-24 kcal)

- **Remove tube:**
  - When 80% of feeds are direct for 24h

- **Additives:**
  - Once on full feeds:
    - 1 Multivitamin 1 mL daily
    - Iron 2-3 mg/kg, daily

- **Feeding:**
  - If MBM supply is low, supplement with preterm formula 22-24 kcal
