__Tip Sheet for HIE/Cooling Patient Management__

__On Admission__

• Document serial Sarnat exams – use \.Sarnat in EPIC

• Request cord gases if you attend delivery of a patient you are considering cooling

• Request placental pathology

• Use NeuroNICU Hypothermia Therapy order set in EPIC

• Admission Labs: Blood Gas, CBC w/diff, Complete Metabolic Panel, CK, Coagulation Panel\. These need to be sent on

admission\.

• aEEG/vEEG to be ordered and placed within 2 hours of admission\. Do not delay for line placement or HUS\.

__Day of Life 1__

• NPO: assent parents to DHM

• Total fluids at 80 mL/kg/day \(UAC \+ UVC\)

• Start TPN via UVC/PICC/PIV w/ AA 3 gm/kg, GIR at 4\- 6 mg/kg/min, IL at 1 gm/kg

• Maintain euglycemia; follow glucose levels closely & correct hypoglycemia

• Monitor Na, K, HCO3, Cl, Cr, Mg, iCa, and Alb & correct PRN w/ Na goal >134, K 3\.5\-5\.0, Cl >97, Mg 2\.0\-2\.4, iCa >1\.1,

Alb >2\.9

• Correct acidosis by addressing possible causes: volume replacement with 10 ml/kg NS or blood products as indicated\.

Consider cardiac dysfunction as cause for acidosis\. Use ½ Na Acetate in chaser fluids\. Avoid sodium bicarbonate\. Consider

THAM instead of sodium bicarbonate\.

• Monitor urine output closely, especially if Cr > 0\.8: consider bladder POCUS & foley placement

• Maintain normotension

• Correct coagulopathy\. Repeat coagulation panel 1\-2 hours after blood products are administered

• Trend daily Cr, AST, ALT, Alb, CK, CRP until results normalize or trending in desired direction

• Switch gentamicin to ceftazidime if Cr elevated or there is oliguria or anuria

• Consider starting aminophylline if decreased urine output or low renal NIRS

• Meningitic antibiotic dosing if seizures present or concern for meningitis

__Day of Life 2__

• After 24 hours of life, if no acidosis and no inotropic support start trophic feedings of EHM/DHM 5 mLs q 3hr PO/Gavage

• Total fluids 100 mL/kg/day including feedings if adequate urine output

• Continue TPN and increase AA and IL and adjust TPN GIR to 5\-8 mg/kg/min and maintain euglycemia

• Monitor electrolytes and correct PRN w/ Na goal >134, K 3\.5\-5\.0, Cl >98, Mg 2\.0\-2\.4, iCa >1\.1, Alb >2\.8

• Monitor for acidosis & consider switching chaser fluids from ½ Na Acetate to ½ NS when CO2 >24

• Maintain normotension and correct coagulopathies

• Trend daily Cr, AST, ALT, Alb, CK until results normalize or trending in desired direction

• Consider starting aminophylline if decreased urine output or low renal NIRS

• Meningitic antibiotic dosing if seizures present or concern for meningitis

__Day of Life 3__

• Increase feeds to 10 mLs q 3 hr

• Total fluids 120 mL/kg/day including feedings if adequate urine output

• Continue TPN and maximize AA and IL, adjusting TPN GIR to 6\-9 mg/kg/min & maintain euglycemia

• Monitor and correct for electrolyte imbalance, acidosis, hypotension

• consider switching chaser fluids from ½ Na Acetate to ½ NS when CO2 >23

__Day of Life 4__

• Discontinue morphine infusion at start of rewarming

• Advance feeds by 20 ml/kg or as tolerated

• Post\-rewarming MRI to be done between day 4 and 7, goal is day 4\-5 for optimal utility of Diffusion Weighted Imaging\. Call

radiology to schedule MRI\. MRI to be scheduled for when rewarming is complete and temperature is stable\.
